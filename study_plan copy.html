Phase Three
3
Exam Period
Rolling Preparation
1 May – 21 May 2026 · Study leave · Physics woven throughout · Sundays still off

Each primary subject gets a dedicated morning prep block before its sitting. Physics is woven into every afternoon from 2 May, building over two weeks of side-session preparation rather than one panicked evening. Sundays remain rest days throughout — the routine that got him here is the routine that carries him through.

Exercise this phase
5km run — every evening as normal throughout, including the night before each exam. Routine beats rest for sleep quality.
Exam day evenings — drop to a 3km easy jog. Keep the habit, reduce the load.
Stick & ball — midday break daily. Keep it going right through to the last exam.
Sundays — full rest as always. No study, no running.
After 21 May — back to full training. Well earned.

Ground Rules
◈
Pomodoro Always50 mins on, 10 mins off. Stick and ball fits perfectly into a long midday break.
◈
Run Every EveningThe 5km is non-negotiable on study days. It clears the day's load and helps sleep.
◈
Mark Every PaperAn unmarked past paper is worthless. The correction session is where the actual improvement happens.
◈
Sunday = OffEvery single week, including during exams. No study, no structured exercise. Non-negotiable.
◈
Exam Eve RoutineNight before every exam: light notes review, 5km run, early night. Same routine every time.
◈
School Weeks CountEvening sessions are shorter but they keep every subject alive. One subject per night, no switching.

8 weeks. 5 subjects. One shot. Make it count.
